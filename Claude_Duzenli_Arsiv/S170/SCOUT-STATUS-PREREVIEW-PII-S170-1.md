## 2 · False-positive traps (factory Turkish)
- CHANCE PASS RATES: Luhn admits ~1 in 10 random digit strings, and the TCKN checksum (two check digits, first digit ≠ 0) ~1 in 100. "Zero false positives on the hard-negative set" is therefore true BY CONSTRUCTION if the negatives are checksum-failing numbers. That proves the fixture, not the detector. Real 16-digit lot or serial codes will hit the card class at ~10%.
- GROUPING: IBAN is normally written in 4-groups ("TR12 0006 …") and cards as 4-4-4-4. But a space-tolerant digit run will also merge adjacent quantities ("1 250 000 3 400") into a 13–19 digit string that passes Luhn 1 time in 10. The SD1 whitespace-grouped-thousands rule is the same hazard.
- PHONE vs ORDER NO: an 11-digit order number starting "05" ("Sipariş 05321234567") is shape-identical to a TR mobile. A landline "0 212 …" collides with grouped quantities.
- IPv4 vs equipment: factory notes carry PLC/HMI addresses (192.168.x.x) and part codes "10.20.30.40". Whether a machine address is PERSONAL data is an owner ruling, not a detector fact.
- NAMES THAT ARE PLANT WORDS: Demir (iron), Çelik (steel), Kaya, Bakır, Kurt, Aslan, Tuna, Akın, Yıldız, Deniz, Kale, Umut, Barış, Güneş. Demir and Çelik are among the most common surnames AND core manufacturing nouns. Casing needs tr-TR folding (DEMİR/demir, I/ı, İ/i).
- DATES "01.10.2026" and times are 3-part, not IPv4, but "1.2.3.4"-style revision codes are.
