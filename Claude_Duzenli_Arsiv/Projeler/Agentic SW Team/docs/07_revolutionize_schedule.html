1 · Timeline — 8 phases, 12–15 months (projection)
The central scheduling truth: implementation-bound phases compress 50–80% with AI assistance; calendar-bound phases (shadow validation, telemetry maturity, production canaries) compress 0%. Hatched bars are calendar-bound and will not move no matter how fast the team is. This is a projection, not a commitment — phases are completion-gated, not date-gated.

2 · Phase 1 detail — the patched foundation backlog
Phase 1 is the only phase with a fully decomposed stage backlog (the rest are produced just-in-time). ~42 stages, 6–8 weeks. Stage groups 1.4 and 1.7 run in parallel after 1.3, which is why the patch adds a net of +1 stage but does not move the Phase-1 calendar. Cadence: 3–5 stages/week ramping to 5–10/week. The bottleneck is human review — every PR gets ≥30 min, and verification does not compress.

3 · Phases 2–8 — goals, outcomes, bound-type
Detailed stage backlogs for these phases are produced just-in-time (when the team is ready to execute), to capture learnings between stages — so they are described here at the phase level by design, not omission.
Why Phases 7 and 8 cannot start early. Self-improvement (P7) needs cells generating real production data — it depends on P6, not the calendar. Meta-cognition (P8) genuinely needs 12+ months of accumulated telemetry from P2 onward before its outputs are reliable (the P2 → P8 dependency). Compressing either ships lower-quality v2 components. This is the single most-misunderstood part of the plan.
