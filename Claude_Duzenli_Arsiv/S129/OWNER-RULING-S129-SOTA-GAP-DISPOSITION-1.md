## THE RULING

> **G-4 önkoşul, diğer 3'ü v1.1** — the owner, choosing the Architect's recommended disposition.

1. **`G-4` is named a PREREQUISITE.** The reverse query *"why was tool X not offered?"* is the
   instrument that makes a routing fault findable at all. S128's diagnosis ran seven screens
   across two products for want of it, and API-Bank cannot be measured honestly by a factory that
   cannot ask its own router why a tool was absent. It stays in v1 as a prerequisite, not as a
   criterion.
2. **`G-1b`, `G-3` and `G-6` go to v1.1**, on the **R8** precedent (`RULE26-HARDEN-1`, owner:
   *"boş beleş iş yapmanın kimseye faydası yok; işe yarayınca çalışmalı"*). They make diagnosis
   more comfortable; they prove nothing.
3. **Nothing is exempted.** Every one of the nine gaps now has a disposition on the record:
   carded, prerequisite, v1.1, or already resolved (`G-8`, the `offeredCount`/`offeredToolNames`
   collision).
