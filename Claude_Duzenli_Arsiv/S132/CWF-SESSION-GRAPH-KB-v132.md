## Numbers, carriers, and the class that owns this factory (S120–S132)

- **[S122] Nothing gates a number on its way into a carrier.** The dominant failure mode is a count crossing from one document to another without re-derivation, and every actor commits it — the owner included, by his own record. `F-S122-STALE-COUNT-CLASS-IS-SUBSTRATE-INDEPENDENT-1`.
- **[S122] Architect precision decays sharply the moment delivery pressure lifts, and the decay is invisible from inside** — nine of fifteen defects in the ninety minutes after the program shipped. The working mitigation is MECHANICAL (a scout window per card), never moral.
- **[S130] A count from memory is the same class in the Architect's own hand** — "20 existing cases", file holds 18.
- **[S120] Two lenses are independent only when they differ in what they ASSUME**, not in the mechanism they run; a filename search and a content grep for the same word are one lens.
- **[S120] Before reporting anything of the owner's as missing, open every place he has already given you** — a wrong finding that reaches the archive is worse than none, because the next session trusts the archive instead of re-measuring.
- **[S124] Seven SOTA items fell off the ledger and the owner caught it from memory** — an ordered list is a carrier too, and a carrier nobody re-derives loses names. `F-S124-SOTA-QUEUE-ITEMS-OFF-LEDGER-1`.
- **[S132] A TRACK can drop documents the way a document drops names** — no register or KB for fifteen sessions; every S117 item not picked up by a card existed only in the S117 files the owner pointed at. The restoration is by name; the discipline is: **every close mints register + KB + close + bootstrap, or the session is not closed.**
- **[S119] An anchor stamped before the last act is stale on arrival — mint the bootstrap LAST.**
- **[S131] A claim carried from an older tree is the stale-fact class** — "the trunk gates" were nightly, not per-push.

## Gates, cards, and the adversary (S121–S132)

- **[S120] A card that cannot name the acceptance criterion it advances is not cut** — 43 of 47 S120 cards were factory self-maintenance; the binding rule would have prevented them.
- **[S121] A scout GREEN is a second lens, never a transfer of responsibility — and it caught something in every round.** [S125] **Every card carries a scout verdict or a named bypass.**
- **[S132] Every Architect card is reviewed by the scout BEFORE a producer acts; the producer card is GATED on a `RELEASE-<name>` row naming its version; the verdict is one bus row; amendments are verbatim replacement sentences carried character-for-character; the old version is VOID.** Nine rounds, two card lines, eight defect classes caught that the producer would have built: SSRF redirect, SSOT collision, false premise, data publication through a CI artefact, silent arming of existing checks, a signal barking on the majority, a scope list missing a render hidden by an `as` assertion, FALSIFIER self-contradiction.
- **[S132] After the first two rounds every FAIL was on the Architect's own prose, never on a carried scout sentence** — the amendments are safe; the Architect's connective tissue is where defects live.
- **[S132] A gate that a lane holds at by ENDING ITS TURN is a gate only a human can open** — the HOLD/RELEASE design assumed the lane keeps ticking; it did not. The gate is a WAIT state, not a terminal state. `F-S132-PRODUCER-TICK-LOOP-ENDS-AT-GATE-1`.
- **[S121/S132] Every dispatch is asynchronous; the Architect must set its OWN tick** (PB-S121-2) — recurred in S132 with the scout's verdicts: the owner asked "timer kurdun mu?" and the answer was no. A wait without a named timer is the "bir ara okur" class S102-YASA-2 forbids.
- **[S121] A ruling told to the owner and relayed to no lane is a stale order in flight.**
- **[S123] "Nothing else changed" stopped being a claim a reviewer audits and became a property of how the artefact was made** — reviewed, preflighted and dispatched bytes are one object by construction (`INSERT … RETURNING sha256` equal to the local file; twenty-two cards in S132, zero drift).
- **[S123] A card must be able to announce its own expiry — watch the ref that actually moves**, not master.
- **[S123] A card is ACTED iff a declared deliverable exists on origin** — a landing without a report is NOT ACTED.
- **[S129] Routing around a working refusal is forbidden — cut an amend card, never rule a gate exception.** Landing must name BOTH refusal classes, AUTHOR-UNKNOWN and SELF-LAND.
- **[S130] Three refusals with an overlapping set is a template defect, not three accidents; every card run on a disarmed gate is PLATINUM-class debt.** [S131] The template + preflight discipline holds: every card GREEN before mint.
- **[S131] Adding a grammar header can turn 1 refusal into 29.** [S132] CP-1 reads "ORDER B.6 tests" as the counted noun "6 tests" — a section reference next to a plural is a count to the grammar.
- **[S122] CP-8 exemption is keyed on a fence's ROLE (anchored by a CLAIMS row or not), never its name.** [S129] CP-8 tolerates a full 40-hex in prose; CP-1 does not — hex lives in fences and CLAIMS cells.
- **[S126] CP-2 cannot tell MENTION from USE; CP-11 is vacuously green on money cards** (no AWS verbs).
- **[S132] A raw stream is never a CI artefact** — stderr from organic turns carries entity surfaces; only counts and statuses cross into a run log, and even the filtered prefixes are read for values before they are allowed.
- **[S132] A silent arming is the obvious implementation** — reusing a parser that feeds existing checks arms them on a new population with no line of those checks changing and no test to notice; the cure is a shape that cannot reach them (toolName-only meta).
- **[S132] A signal that fires on the majority of lawful traffic teaches its reader to ignore it** — worse than no signal; one distinguishing outcome name is the cure.
- **[S132] A type assertion launders a new union member** — a third value added at the declaration raises no compile error at a render that narrows with `as`, and throws at runtime; the union declared in three files needs all three plus the render in scope.

## Liveness, heartbeats, and the owner's eye (S118–S132)

- **[S118] The word is STOPPED, never "dead"; states are BUSY · STOPPED · LOOP-STOPPED · HUNG; the closing artefact is a STOP CERTIFICATE** (S118-H4).
- **[S120] A heartbeat is a claim by the measured thing about itself; the trunk is an independent witness and outranks it.**
- **[S124/S125/S131] Heartbeats measure ANY reader** — reading another lane's box WRITES that lane's heartbeat (`F-S124-HEARTBEAT-MEASURES-ANY-READER-1`, F-7); every heartbeat is contaminated; measure nothing from them. [S132] The scout reads with `--read`, which stamps nothing.
- **[S122 §4 / S132] LIVENESS IS POSITIVE ONLY: `factory_state.state` is hand-written and fossilises; the heartbeat is quiet while a lane WORKS and fresh while it IDLES** — both indicators are inversely correlated with production. A lane's life is read from OUTPUT only (a landed commit, a pushed branch, a bus row).
- **[S130] Bus silence is not idleness — the owner's clone over the device bridge is a lane-progress sensor.**
- **[S125] Silence read as staleness: the foreman's parked state was RIGHT; the defect was invisibility, not idleness.**
- **[S124] A verbal "it works" is not a live witness; the screenshot falsified it the same hour.** [S127] Anomaly-detector silence is not world silence.
- **[S132] The cold restart's death certificates came from the owner's eye and the machine recorded them UNCORROBORATED** — the S117 design working as designed: the factory NAMES, a human CONFIRMS.
- **[S132] An owner keystroke into a lane window is a nudge, not a cure** — AG-4 correctly refused to post verdicts when the scout's line was pasted into its window; the cure is the boot's tick loop.
